## 完整提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand redesign.
Edit target: the attached rejected Cherry Peek logo. The user dislikes it because it is too abstract and unattractive.
Keep only the core brief: Cherry Peek is a modern desktop Git history browser; two cherry fruits sit at different heights; a small arrow replaces the leaf. Red logo, dark wordmark, genuine transparent background.
Completely redraw the symbol with much better visual taste:
- It must read FIRST as an elegant pair of cherries, SECOND as a subtle branching idea. BOTH fruits are solid red, with softly rounded cherry silhouettes and a tiny natural dimple where each stem enters; no ring, no donut, no eye.
- The left cherry is lower, the right cherry higher by about half a fruit diameter. Bring the two fruits closer together so this is one compact mark. They should feel like one pair, not two distant endpoints.
- Use two slender, beautifully tensioned curved stems, about one-fifth of the fruit diameter, tapering subtly into a single simple shared top. Smooth diagonal arcs rather than orthogonal pipes. Remove the central round junction entirely. NO extra circle at the fork.
- At the upper tip, form ONE tiny right-up arrow from the end of the stem. The arrowhead is understated, sharp and integrated; it occupies no more than one-fifth of a fruit diameter. It should feel like a clever botanical tip, not a business growth chart or upload symbol. No leaf.
- Overall symbol is compact, slightly asymmetrical, sophisticated, distinctive and easy to recognize at small size.
Typography: exact text "Cherry Peek", medium-weight refined contemporary sans serif, airy but precise kerning, calmer and smaller than the rejected heavy lettering. Keep symbol above the wordmark with a healthy gap, and generous balanced outer margins on a compact square canvas.
Use perfectly flat warm cherry-red shapes and flat charcoal type; clean vector-like edges. The character should come from silhouette and spacing, not effects.
Avoid the rejected look: no organization chart, no headset shape, no horizontal branch bar, no blocky elbow tubing, no central node, no ring, no oversized arrow, no fat bubbly lettering. Also no gradients, lighting, shadows, bevels, shiny fruit, textures, extra text, tagline, frame or mockup. Preserve real alpha transparency.
Produce one decisive, polished identity concept, not a collage or variations sheet.
```
